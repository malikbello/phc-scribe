# Labelling quickstart (5 minutes to read)

For the team members who label benchmark items. The full rules are in `docs/annotation-guidelines.md`; this page is enough to start.

## What you receive
- `labeler.html`: the labelling tool. It runs in your browser (Chrome is best), offline. **Nothing you type is sent anywhere.**
- A queue file such as `label_queue.calibration.jsonl`: the items to label.

The items are confidential. Don't forward them or post them anywhere.

## Steps
1. Open `labeler.html` in Chrome (on a phone: save it, then open it from Files).
2. Type your name in the top bar.
3. Choose the queue file under **Queue**.
4. For each item, read the text. For every finding about **the pregnant woman herself**:
   1. Select the exact words that show it (press and hold, then drag, on a phone).
   2. Choose the finding from the list.
   3. Tap **present**, **absent**, **possible** or **historical** (past pregnancy).
   4. Tap **Add finding**.
5. Tap **Save & next**. If there's nothing to record, tap Save & next with no findings; that's a valid answer.
6. If an item isn't about pregnancy, or can't be understood, pick a reason and tap **Skip**.
7. When you're done (or stopping for the day), tap **Export labels** and send the downloaded file to Malik.

Your work is saved in the browser as you go. To continue on another day, open the same queue file; if you changed device or browser, also load your last exported file under **Resume labels**.

## The five rules that matter most
1. **Record what is said, not what you'd diagnose.** "Headache and BP 150/100" gives *headache*, not "pre-eclampsia".
2. **Include the cue in the evidence** for absent, possible and historical findings: "no bleeding", not just "bleeding".
3. **Severe only if the text says so** (severe, very bad, persistent, "gidigidi"). Otherwise use the mild finding.
4. **Past pregnancy = historical.** "Bled in her last pregnancy, no bleeding now" gives two findings: historical and absent.
5. **When torn between two readings, choose the one a cautious clinician would act on.** Use *possible* rather than leaving a danger sign out.

About 1 minute per item is normal. Don't agonise; write a note in the Note box if unsure.
